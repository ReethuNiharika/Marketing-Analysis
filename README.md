# Marketing Intelligence Dashboard

## Executive Summary

The Marketing Intelligence Dashboard is a comprehensive analytics platform that unifies advertising data from Facebook, Google, and TikTok into a single, actionable view. This enterprise-grade solution provides real-time performance tracking, cross-channel comparison, and data-driven strategic recommendations.

### Key Findings

**Facebook** emerges as the most cost-effective channel with the lowest CPA at $7.64. This represents the most efficient customer acquisition across all platforms.

**Google Shopping** delivers exceptional return on ad spend at 6.3x, significantly exceeding the target benchmark of 4.8x by 30%. This channel provides the highest immediate revenue return per dollar spent.

**TikTok** shows strong engagement potential with 13,000 total conversions, though the video completion rate of 25% indicates opportunity for creative optimization.

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Budget Reallocation - Facebook to TikTok

**Current Situation:** Facebook CPA at $7.64, TikTok CPA at $11.00

**Recommendation:** Shift 15% of Facebook budget to TikTok to capitalize on higher conversion volume. The reallocation is expected to deliver +12% incremental conversions at the same spend level.

**Projected Impact:** +18% ROI improvement

### 2. Google Shopping Expansion

**Current Situation:** Google Shopping ROAS at 6.3x exceeds target by 30%

**Recommendation:** Increase Shopping campaign budget by 20% and expand to competitor brand terms and high-intent keywords to capture additional market share.

**Projected Impact:** $2.5 million additional revenue

### 3. Creative Optimization for TikTok

**Current Situation:** Video completion rate at 25% indicates significant drop-off

**Recommendation:** Implement A/B testing framework for first 3 seconds of video content. Add strong calls-to-action at 25% and 50% watch time markers. Test different hook styles and content formats.

**Projected Impact:** +15-20% improvement in retention rate
